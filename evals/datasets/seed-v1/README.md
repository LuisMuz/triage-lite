# seed-v1

Frozen bilingual (ES/EN) evaluation set for TriageLite's classify and dedupe stages. Never edit these files: `MANIFEST.json` holds their SHA-256 hashes and `make eval-validate` fails on any change. Corrections go into a new version (`seed-v2`).

## Contents
- `incidents.jsonl`: one `RawIncident` per line (`external_id`, `title`, `description`, `steps`, `environment`, `reported_at`), in the same shape the pipeline ingests from CSV.
- `labels.jsonl`: gold labels per incident: `lang`, `severity`, `category`, `component`, `duplicate_group`, `near_miss_of`, `source`, `split`.
- `MANIFEST.json`: the coverage rules (`spec`), file hashes and counts.

## How it was made
1. **Candidates (synthetic).** 140 incidents for a fictional e-commerce app (TiendaLite), generated by Claude (Opus 5.5) in a Claude Code session on 2026-10-29: 70 ES / 70 EN; per language 14 critical, 20 high, 20 medium, 16 low; 22 duplicate groups of 2–4 reports (8 cross-language); 13 near-misses (same component, different bug). Deliberately messy: typos, Spanglish, vague titles, pasted stack traces. All data is fictional.
2. **Human review.** Every label was checked by hand against `config/taxonomy.toml`. Weak or ambiguous candidates were dropped.
3. **Hand-written items.** At least 10 adversarial incidents written by hand (typos, sarcasm, two bugs in one report) replaced synthetic ones.
4. **Split.** By duplicate group, never by row: a whole group lands in one split. 60 `tuning` / 60 `holdout`, roughly stratified by severity × language.

## Rules
- Tune prompts on `tuning` only. Run `holdout` once, for the reported number.
- Labels inside a duplicate group are identical (same bug, same labels).

## Known limitations
Final counts: 120 items (60/60); 22 duplicate groups with 52 members, 9 of them cross-language; 9 near-misses. Severity × language: critical 10 ES / 12 EN, high 17 / 17, medium 17 / 19, low 13 / 15. 10 groups in `tuning`, 12 in `holdout`.

- **Gold labels are mostly the generator's.** The reviewer changed 1 of 110 synthetic labels (`cand-110`, kept as `crash` after review). Agreement this high means the eval partly measures Claude against Claude-proposed labels; borderline severities (high vs critical, medium vs low) deserve a second human pass in `seed-v2`.
- **One duplicate was found only by reading.** `hand-001` (EN) and `cand-082` (ES) describe the same bug and were joined as `dg-23` during review. The validator checks structure, not meaning: other unlabeled duplicates may remain and would count as false positives in the dedupe eval.
- **Hand-written items are realistic but not adversarial** (no sarcasm, heavy typos or two-bugs-in-one reports), so they test ordinary phrasing rather than robustness.
- **Critical ES is the thinnest cell (10).** Per-class F1 for `critical` will have wide confidence intervals.
